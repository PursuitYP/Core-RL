# Parallel Worker Audit: Report Depth And Figures, Round 2

Scope inspected: `final/reports/proposals/` and `final/reports/integrated/`, with emphasis on final-facing `report.md`, `report_zh.md`, READMEs, and report-local generated figures.

Severity key:

- Critical: likely to mislead final selection or violate the independent-report contract.
- High: likely to weaken a final submission unless fixed.
- Medium: visible quality/reproducibility issue, but not fatal if the topic is not selected.
- Low: polish issue.

## Suggested Triage Order

1. Fix the README/index inconsistency and independence overcount first. This affects final topic selection more than any single figure.
2. For the strongest likely final candidates, add compact headline result tables before dense figures: Reward-Centered Sarsa, Output-Controlled TD, Continual Dyna Model Aging, Scale-Invariant Continuing Control, Useful Predictive Knowledge.
3. Demote or label subset diagnostics clearly: Centered TD Diagnostics, Dyna Planning Budget, GVF Predictive State, and other single-curve diagnostics if a larger related report is selected.
4. Bring Chinese reports to parity only for reports that remain final-facing; do not spend translation effort on reports that will be archived or explicitly supporting-only.
